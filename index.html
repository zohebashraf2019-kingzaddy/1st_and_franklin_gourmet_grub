OUR STORY

Family-built. Flavor-driven.

Food truck season is in full swing across Northern Nevada, and 1st & Franklin Gourmet Grub is building a loyal following with Southern-inspired comfort food and family recipes.

Owner John Franklin brings a menu designed around generous portions, bold flavor and the kind of food that brings people together. From catfish and ribs to crawfish étouffée and alligator sausage, every stop is about serving Reno and Sparks with something memorable.
